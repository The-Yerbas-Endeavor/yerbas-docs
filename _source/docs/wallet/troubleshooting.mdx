# Troubleshooting 4.0.1.4

## Synchronization and startup

Confirm the executable reports 4.0.1.4, the correct network/data directory is selected, the disk has space, and only one process is using the directory. Inspect `debug.log`, peer connections and the exact error message.

```bash
./yerbas-cli getblockchaininfo
./yerbas-cli getnetworkinfo
./yerbas-cli getpeerinfo
```

Automatic recovery-history indexing may take time. A routine upgrade does not require `-reindex`. Missing block history or recovery indexes can require restoring compatible history or reindexing; follow the diagnostic and retain your original data and wallet backups first. Pruned nodes may not have the blocks needed for migration.

Do not delete `sporks.dat`, `evodb`, `llmq`, wallet files or all `.dat` files as a generic repair. See [recorded recovery](/docs/smartnodes/recovery/).

## Wallet history or balances

Check synchronization and the selected wallet first. A rescan rebuilds wallet transaction history from available blocks; it is different from rebuilding chainstate. After backing up and stopping the node, `./yerbasd -rescan` is the startup rescan option. Keep the same data directory; do not run a second process against it. Pruned blocks may need restoration.

A seed/private-key restore does not recover every local asset reward or payout-job record. Use a complete wallet backup where available. Never share your wallet file, seed, passphrase, operator secret or signing key when requesting support.
